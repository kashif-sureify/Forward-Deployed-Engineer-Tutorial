Question: According to the text, if a prime number N divides a product ab, what must be true about a and b?
Answer: According to the text, if a prime number $N$ divides a product of two numbers ($ab \equiv 0 \pmod N$), then it divides at least one of the two numbers ($a \equiv 0 \pmod N$ or $b \equiv 0 \pmod N$).
